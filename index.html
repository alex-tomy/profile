About

Industry experience.
Academic purpose.

I am a Senior Lecturer in Information Technology and Internet of Things at La Trobe University, Bendigo. Before moving into academia, I spent seven years in software engineering and software industry roles, building computational approaches and intelligent systems. My teaching and leadership work focuses on industry-relevant learning, curriculum development and preparing students for successful careers.

Research expertise

My research develops and evaluates data-driven approaches and intelligent solutions to improve data quality, detect anomalies, optimise system performance and support informed decision-making. I am especially interested in applications in healthcare, autonomous systems and intelligent infrastructure.

Research

Reliable data.
Smarter decisions.

My research combines computational methods, data analytics and applied AI to address complex, incomplete, heterogeneous and continuously generated data. Established work includes stream data quality, anomaly detection, IoT analytics, predictive modelling and intelligent software systems.

Developing research directions

Where I’m looking next.

These are emerging directions described in my research portfolio. They are research interests and project opportunities, not all represented as funded or currently active projects.

DIRECTION / HEALTH

Health & wellbeing analytics

Data-driven work on older populations, regional and rural communities, environmental influences, health planning and resource allocation.

DIRECTION / SAFETY

Autonomous systems assurance

AI-supported safety analysis, risk assessment and simulation-based generation and testing of rare or high-risk scenarios.

DIRECTION / ENVIRONMENT

Intelligent environmental monitoring

Combining robotics, IoT sensors and analytics for applications such as water-quality assessment and monitoring platforms.

DIRECTION / CAREERS

Career & skills intelligence

Connecting skills, education pathways and labour-market information to support career exploration and informed decisions.

DIRECTION / TRUST

Trustworthy, context-aware AI

Exploring explainable and trustworthy methods for complex data settings, including the use of large language models.

RESEARCH APPROACH

From methods to use

Developing and evaluating computational methods, integrating context, and translating outcomes through interdisciplinary and industry collaboration.

Funded work includes the Australian Space Industry Skills Gap Analysis Project Grant with SmartSat CRC (Lead Chief Investigator, 2021–2022), alongside La Trobe teaching and learning grants.
